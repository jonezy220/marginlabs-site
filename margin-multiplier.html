Why this is independent

Vendor-neutral. Operator-built.

The Margin Multiplier is built by Margin Labs, an independent advisory practice focused on embedded payments for vertical SaaS. We don't take kickbacks from processors. We don't have a preferred PayFac partner. The output is what real platforms capture, not what a sales team wants you to see.

Basis-point ranges reflect what we've seen across hundreds of platform conversations, processor RFPs and PE diligence files. The recommendation is based on your inputs, not a predetermined path that ends with a vendor sale.

Frequently asked

What people actually want to know before they run it.

How accurate are these basis-point ranges?

The bps ranges reflect what real platforms capture at each model, calibrated across hundreds of conversations, processor RFPs and PE diligence files. They're directional, not precise. Your actual capture depends on vertical, merchant mix, activation rate and the specific terms you negotiate with a processor.

What's the difference between PayFac-lite and Full PayFac?

PayFac-lite means you use a partner like Stripe, Adyen, Tilled or Finix who handles the underwriting and money movement under their PayFac license. You get a meaningful slice of the economics with low operational burden. Full PayFac means you become the payment facilitator yourself, with sponsor bank relationships, your own compliance program and the full economic upside. Higher revenue, materially higher complexity and capital requirements. Read more on PayFac-as-a-Service →

Why do you ask for my email before showing results?

Two reasons. First, we send a copy of your results plus the free Embedded Payments Guide PDF to your inbox so you have a reference document. Second, it lets us send the occasional follow-up if you want to go deeper. No sales call. No spam. Unsubscribe link in every email.

Is the free Embedded Payments Guide actually free?

Yes. It's a vendor-neutral PDF that covers the four monetization models, basis-point math, processor economics, merchant activation rate and the operational considerations for each path. No payment required. No gated upsell. The Guide is the short version of our paid Strategic Decision Framework.

Do you share my data with processors or sell it?

No. We don't sell your data. We don't share your inputs or email with processors, PayFac partners or any third party. The data stays with Margin Labs. If a meaningful next step involves a partner introduction, we ask first and you decide.

What if my current setup is more complex than the four standard models?

Pick the closest model and read the results as directional. If you're running a hybrid setup, a sponsor bank residual structure or a custom interchange-plus arrangement, the Multiplier gives you the order of magnitude but a Quick Start Call is the right next step to model your specific situation.

What's the next step after I get my results?

Depends on the gap. If the opportunity is meaningful and you want to move, the Strategic Decision Framework ($139) is a vendor-neutral playbook that takes you from question to decision. If you want a focused working session with model confirmation, processor shortlist and warm introductions, the Quick Start Call ($379) is the right path. We'll tell you which one fits your situation.

How does this compare to talking to a processor directly?

Processors will quote you their model. We model all four side by side, including the ones they don't sell. The Multiplier is a position-checker before you take a sales meeting, not a substitute for one. Most platforms run it and then go into vendor conversations with a much clearer picture of what's possible.
